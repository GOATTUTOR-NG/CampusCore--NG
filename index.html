CSET Smart Timetable

Search a course code and find the day and time listed in the provisional CSET timetable. No more zooming into eight PDF pages like you're investigating a crime scene.

Important timetable notice: This searchable index is based on the College of Science, Engineering and Technology (CSET) provisional 2026/2027 Harmattan Semester timetable dated 10 November 2026. It is for CSET students only; it should not be treated as the timetable for other UNIOSUN colleges. The source document does not provide a verified department-to-course mapping, so this tool searches the listed course codes rather than claiming to generate a complete departmental timetable. Always confirm your schedule and any changes with your department or lecturer.

Level is estimated from the course-code number (e.g. BIO 201 = 200 Level). Confirm your course level with your department where course numbering differs.
